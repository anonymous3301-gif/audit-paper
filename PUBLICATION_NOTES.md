## Known limitations of this draft

- The text is a draft; the final version will be updated.
- H2 order/resource effects are not detected (n=5, Holm m=2 non-significant).
- F1 fails: Kaczmarz does not retain ≥90% of its d=128 capacity at n=128.
- Collapse rate is 24.7% at k=5 (sensitivity 18.8%-45.8%).
- C1'-EQ fails: Kaczmarz is superior, not equivalent.
- The published comparison is task-variant dependent.
- Figures 1-7 are carried over from the earlier manuscript and need
  renumbering/reformatting for the final version.
- The paper is distributed as Markdown (`paper.md`) plus figure PDFs. No
  compiled PDF is included in v0.1-draft.
